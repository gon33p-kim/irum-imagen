# Shot library

Choose a scene family that fits the brief, then customize it. Do not copy every cue into one image.

| Scene family | Micro-action | Reality cues | Camera direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cafe or bakery | Fork bite, wiping a crumb, glass condensation on fingers | Window light, tabletop bounce, crumbs, shallow background activity | 50mm candid at table height |
| Restaurant | Lifting noodles, wrapping food, reacting to steam | Steam catching light, reflections on metal or glass, mixed practical light | 35-50mm mid-shot |
| Home morning | Folding laundry, fixing bedding, pet interruption | White-sheet bounce, rumpled fabric, soft window gradients | 35-50mm handheld |
| Bedroom portrait | Turning toward the window, sitting on a bed edge | Pale-wall bounce, fabric wrinkles, calm posture, gentle highlight roll-off | 50-85mm, eye level |
| Rooftop golden hour | Wind moves bangs or a light garment, spontaneous laugh | Warm rim light, concrete bounce, skyline bokeh | 50mm, slightly tilted candid |
| Street or crosswalk | Adjusting a bag, looking for the signal, stepping forward | Uneven daylight, passing people with mild motion blur, real street depth | 28-35mm documentary |
| Transit | Brief doze, catching balance, hand on rail | Fluorescent color cast, metal reflections, airflow, commuters in soft focus | 35-50mm side angle |
| Rain or building entrance | Shaking an umbrella, reacting to a gust | Wet-ground bounce, droplets, cool overcast light plus warm interior spill | 35-50mm fast snapshot |
| Shop or library | Comparing an item, opening a book, reaching toward a shelf | Practical overhead light, shelf-level perspective, non-readable background details | 35-50mm shoulder or shelf height |
| Screen-lit interior | Removing headphones, turning from the monitor | Dim ambient light plus monitor glow, believable low-light noise | 50mm close portrait |
| Product lifestyle | Opening, pouring, applying, packing, or cleaning the product | Finger pressure, weight, material response, reflections and residue | Focal length chosen for product scale |

## Tasteful sensual direction

For an adult sensual or alluring brief, create mood through material, fit, light, expression, posture, and camera distance. State that the subject is an adult. Useful controls include satin, silk, soft knit, an elegant fitted silhouette, relaxed shoulders, calm confidence, a slight lean, an over-shoulder glance, collarbone edge light, soft shadow gradients, and 85mm background compression. Keep the result tasteful and within the user's requested degree of exposure.

## Variation axes

Change one to three axes per variation:

- place or room zone
- time and light source
- action or reaction
- framing and camera height
- focal length appropriate to the new framing
- wardrobe color or material
- environmental condition such as rain, breeze, steam, or reflected light

Keep identity anchors, age, body proportions, and signature styling stable when the same character must recur.
